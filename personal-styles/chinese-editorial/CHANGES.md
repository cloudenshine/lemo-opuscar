# Changes from Swiss Motion Graphics

| Dimension | Base | Personal version | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palette | Neutral paper and saturated red protagonist | Warm paper and restrained vermilion edit | Editorial tone rather than concert-poster energy |
| Type | Archivo, extreme English scale contrast | Chinese sans-serif with large step numerals | Less extreme display type, more Chinese reading room |
| Story | Five rules build a musical poster | Notes → evidence → editing → judgment | Independent illustrative subject, no fabricated news |
| Motion | Rotate, programme, wall of artifacts | Page states, column alignment, line deletion | Lower camera diversity is retained as a visible gap |
| Sound | Motorik ensemble with voice | Dry print pulses, mallets, no voice | Smaller dependency footprint; less layered performance |

Comparative evidence and unresolved limits: [review](../../studio/comparison/REVIEW.zh-CN.md).

## 1.1.0 — Second comparison round

The artifact rotates out of a stack, columns settle at staggered times, editing pushes in, and a fan of outputs resolves into the final page. Body text enters after the noise lines clear. Page, printing, marker, knife and stamp sounds have different envelopes; chapter-specific bass/mallet/percussion, two silences and four editable buses replace the uniform score. The original still has more extreme typography and more spatial variety.
