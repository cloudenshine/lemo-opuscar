# A Place for Every Idea · 归序工作空间

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

0–6s: original idea cards accumulate beside a short Chinese hook, then hold through a literal silence. 6–12s: records enter fixed workspace destinations. 12–18s: a soft band reveals the resolved interface; a pointer approaches the task card, clicks at 13.5s, and produces a confirmation at 14s. 18–24s: one workspace becomes the final idea. The interface is fictional and the number is explicitly not a benchmark.

Canvas scene: [demo/scene.ts](demo/scene.ts). Sound events drive synthesized glass ticks and pulse; the melody and interface differ from the upstream Tidy film. No real brand or OS chrome is drawn.

From the repository root: `node studio/src/cli.ts create product-demo --style dark-product`, then `node studio/src/cli.ts render product-demo`. Output is `films/product-demo/out/film.mp4`.
