# Dark Product Studio · 深色科技与产品演示

Based on Dark Tech Keynote (`dark-keynote`) from [Lemo-Opuscar by Lemomo (LemoLab)](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar). Original Chinese concept interface and score. Documentation: [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/), with the additional filmmaking permission in the repository LICENSE.

## 1. Essence
A software interface demonstrates one useful verb. A dark stage, coherent UI surfaces and precise action reveal the promise. Not a real screen recording, glass hardware render or generic neon HUD.
## 2. Materials
Near-black `#090C13`, cool radial lift, 48px hairline grid, three dark surface values and restrained top edges. All cards and chrome are original. No traffic-light buttons, copied app controls or brand logos.
## 3. Colour
Neutral cool greys plus one blue-violet accent `#A4B2FF`. Reserve it for a confirmation or the final single number. Do not colour every card. The original's lime is a reference choice, not a requirement.
## 4. Type
Local Chinese sans-serif, full glyph coverage, no redistributed proprietary fonts. One short headline per state. Product labels remain large enough to read; the final number is explained explicitly and is not a fabricated performance result. Original-concept disclosure remains visible.
## 5. Motion
24fps, no random per-frame noise. Short typed entrances land on whole-frame subdivisions. Cards settle into assigned slots, not nearest-neighbour flicker. Freeze at the decision; a soft light band reveals the resolved workspace.
## 6. Camera
The starter uses a wide information stage, a framed workspace and a large-number close composition. A pointer approaches a task card, clicks at 13.5s, and triggers an archive confirmation at 14s. It does not yet match the original's 2.5D depth, multi-shot inserts or expressive cursor acting. Add those only when motivated by the new brief.
## 7. Sound
Clean synthesized glass tones, low pulse, a quiet pad, short interface ticks. Spawn/settle/reveal/collapse cues are shared through EV. Silence 5–6s clears attention before the resolved state. No voice is added without a narration brief.
## 8. Native moves
Notes accumulate, assigned destinations establish order, a soft band reveals the product, one large number summarises the promise. The count denotes one workspace, not measured speed or adoption.
## 9. Pitfalls
Avoid unreadable low-contrast grey, false stats, hard white glints, copycat interface controls and leaving a generic placeholder rectangle as the hero. Do not confuse an animation of a concept with a functioning SaaS product.
## 10. Engine
[demo/scene.ts](demo/scene.ts), original Canvas2D. The generated interface is deterministic and exposes the upstream render contract. Per-project score source is snapshotted. The playback and export toolchain is shared with the other personal styles.
## 11. Variation
Different briefs can reveal a release checklist, a research library, an approval workflow or an empty editor. Rebuild the product verb and narrative. Keep the style, not this demo's cards and 24-second route.
