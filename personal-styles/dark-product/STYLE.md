# Dark Product Studio · 深色科技与产品演示

Based on Dark Tech Keynote (`dark-keynote`) from [Lemo-Opuscar by Lemomo (LemoLab)](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar). Original Chinese concept interface and score. Documentation: [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/), with the additional filmmaking permission in the repository LICENSE.

## 1. Essence
A software interface demonstrates one useful verb. A dark stage, coherent UI surfaces and precise action reveal the promise. Not a real screen recording, glass hardware render or generic neon HUD.
## 2. Materials
Near-black `#090C13`, cool radial lift, 48px hairline grid, three dark surface values and restrained top edges. All cards and chrome are original. No traffic-light buttons, copied app controls or brand logos.
## 3. Colour
Neutral cool greys plus one blue-violet accent `#A4B2FF`. Reserve it for selection, contact, confirmation or a resolved relationship. Do not colour every card. The original's lime is a reference choice, not a requirement.
## 4. Type
Local Chinese sans-serif, full glyph coverage, no redistributed proprietary fonts. One short headline per state. Product labels remain large enough to read; claims must be explained explicitly and must not fabricate performance results. Original-concept disclosure remains visible.
## 5. Motion
24fps, no random per-frame noise. Short typed entrances land on whole-frame subdivisions. Cards settle into assigned slots, not nearest-neighbour flicker. Freeze at the decision; a soft light band reveals the resolved workspace.
## 6. Camera
Version 1.4.0 depth-sorts 108 projected cards across four layers with an independent camera rig. All 107 background records retain identities through batch routing, side banks and a final grid; they are abstract symbols, not individually readable text. One identified task card is selected, dragged into a library, expanded for source inspection and retained in the final workspace. The cursor shares its contact point throughout the drag, releases before inspection and visibly triggers the search result. The rig combines yaw, pitch, truck, crane and dolly segments, then locks for reading. Shared projection governs the hero, library and background; the pointer stays on the hero billboard plane. Keep the entire hero inside the stage, clip background at the stage boundary and use depth-based blur only as a focus approximation. These are Canvas billboard projections, not a general 3D renderer.
## 7. Sound
Glass arrivals, slot snaps, a dry click, a two-note confirmation and a low collapse impulse carry distinct functions. Four harmonic regions follow accumulation, ordering, inspection and resolution; the inspection drops the pulse. Four editable buses include ambience and transition sweeps, stereo reflections and music ducking. Literal silence at 5–6s and 17.25–18s clears attention. No voice is added without a narration brief.
## 8. Native moves
Notes accumulate, assigned destinations establish order, a soft band reveals the product, a retained task and its relationships resolve the promise. No unverified speed or adoption claims.
## 9. Pitfalls
Avoid unreadable low-contrast grey, false stats, hard white glints, copycat interface controls and leaving a generic placeholder rectangle as the hero. Do not confuse an animation of a concept with a functioning SaaS product.
## 10. Engine
[demo/scene.ts](demo/scene.ts), original Canvas2D. The generated interface is deterministic and exposes the upstream render contract. Per-project score source is snapshotted. The playback and export toolchain is shared with the other personal styles.
## 11. Variation
Different briefs can reveal a release checklist, a research library, an approval workflow or an empty editor. Rebuild the product verb and narrative. Keep the style, not this demo's cards and 24-second route.
